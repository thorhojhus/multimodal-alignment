\section*{Digital Innovation And Transformation}

Larger companies need to innovate and transform, but not all are capeable.

\subsection*{Clarification of Subjects} 

\textbf{Innovation} classical definition is a new combination of new/existing tools, resources etc.\\

New != Innovation, can also be improvements of existing products!\\

\noindent\textbf{Digital:} \textit{attributes:} easy to distribute, easy to replicate.\\

Digital Evolution; from signals - digital innovation can be described as \textit{transformation} and important to digital engineering.\\

Products -\> services in digital\\

\noindent\textbf{Transformation:} existing product as a digital product (watch -\> digital watch). Value usually created with \textit{transformation.}\\

Often develop existing solutions, because that is what the market wants. \\

Ask yourself: what does your user want

\noindent\textbf{User Needs:} to create \textit{value}, how does our tech provide value to user -\> need to involve users and think about their needs!\\

\noindent\textbf{Value}: no innovation without value; from engineering: simple look is economical, so could be Quality divided by Resources\\

There is societal value as well; sustainability value;\\

Value is matter of \textit{mindset} of users and developers.\\

\noindent\textbf{Mindset:} digital development in two dimension: \textit{can I change and adapt to the digital development?} and \textit{will the digital development adapt to me?} as well as \textit{Will I be able to affect in a way so that it suits me?}\\

Four archetypes of users/developers:

So: how do we approach problems? How do we think about them?

In our system, which layer is the most important.\\

\section*{Scalability and Security}
Scalabity from engineering POV - which questions do we ask? Risk and Oppurtunities.

\subsection*{Clarification of Subjects}

Ability of a system to suit needs over time - growing/shrinking over time. Also security and user needs.

\noindent\textbf{Risks \& Oppurtunities}
\textit{Risks}: Availability, Performance, Reliability. \textit{Security Issues:} attack points and holes.\\

Lack of availability or reliability; \textit{removal of features} and lack of availability will \textit{push us away!}.\\

Make them \textit{perform} and \textit{reliable}. Sometimes they are design considerations!\\

\noindent\textbf{Network Effect}: more users can create more value.
Phases: \textit{Cold Start Problem} - \textit{Critical mass} - \textit{Bandwagon / Inflection Point} where lots of users join in.\\

\textbf{Cold Start Problem:} having enough data/users to create value. For example recommendations for users in Netflix!\\

\textbf{Critical mass} enough users that new users can benefit from other.\\

\textbf{Inflection Point} can just increase number of users as long as your system are available and performing.\\

\subsection*{Resource Management}

Handle virtual resources. Scalability, Cost Savings and Flexibility are signs of a digital transformation.

Instagram more value the more users it has (network effects). Allows scaling rapidly. GreenMobility App (moves physical cars to a service of lending cars).

Cloud Computing has \textit{elasticity}; can quickly match resources needed. Also disadvantes: \textit{network latency}, \textit{bad performance of containters}.

Can use \textit{load balancing} to alleviate, and \textit{query theorem}.

\textit{Security} and \textit{privary} is a concern in cloud infrastrucutre, as they share a lot of users. 

\textit{Failure links?} build redundancy, multiple datacenters, more nodes.

\textbf{Green Computing} use renewable energy for data centers, use generated heat smart

\subsection*{Scalability And Security}

\textit{Complexity of systems} grows
as such \textit{Attack Surface} grows leading to
\textit{User Management} issues\\

\noindent\textbf{Complexitiy} as systems grow, more components or instances grow. Harder to to test/verify and maintain. We need to understand the \textit{components} of our system, the \textit{security properties}, the \textit{interactions} and the \textit{potentiel side effects}.\\

\noindent\textbf{Attack Surface} expertan property. Accessible in more ways as it grows. More things can go wrong. \\

\noindent\textbf{User Manamegent} the userbase is larger; who are they, for identification, and their priviliges. These are difficult in large scale systems to manage. Statuses of users can change / stop being users.

We need to \textbf{match the security of enrollment in the system to potential damage of misuse} - which is hard to anticipate.
