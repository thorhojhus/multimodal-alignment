\subsection{Data Processing}

Many of the pre-trained models that we have incorporated into the project experiments were trained on data that had certain requirements. For example, most models were trained on audio data sampled at a rate of 16kHz, but some audio has higher resolution and is recorded at 48kHz.

As the models were trained on 16kHz data, providing them with 48kHz data would not be optimal. It is, however, possible to downsample signals between two different rates with ease\footnote{as long as ratio is a natural number}. Assume in our case, we want to go from 48kHz to 16kHz;  $\frac{48}{16} - 1 = \frac{3}{1} - 1 = 2$ zeros between every point in the original signal. A \textit{low pass filter} is then applied on half of lowest frequency of the two (8kHz). This ensures no aliasing occurs when we downsample the signal. In the example we then sample every 3rd point in the now filtered signal.
